# NEBCA Platform

High-fidelity enterprise governance, compliance and operational assurance prototype.

## Build 001 — Foundation

The current branch establishes the NEBCA browser prototype with:

- secure-style demo login
- executive dashboard
- industry overlay selector
- responsive enterprise navigation
- Governance Control Centre
- Decision Register
- Delegations of Authority
- Policy Governance
- Ownership, Control & Strategic Asset Governance
- Action Accountability
- Board Assurance
- Compliance Control Centre
- Obligation & Condition Register
- Licence & Permit Register
- Evidence Vault
- Regulatory Returns
- CAPA
- Compliance Calendar
- Risk and Audit workspaces
- SSAL and ISO SHEC SMS entry points
- regulator workbench and sealed evidence exposure
- Namibia identity and `Designed in Namibia` branding

## Roadmap

1. Foundation and executive dashboard
2. Governance — in progress
3. Compliance and evidence control — in progress
4. Standards, Safety & Assurance Layer (SSAL)
5. ISO SHEC SMS
6. Risk and audit
7. Executive intelligence and XAI
8. Mining, fisheries, ports/harbour and petroleum overlays
9. Regulator workbench and sealed evidence access

The prototype is deliberately client-facing and contains no sales copy inside operational screens.
