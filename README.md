# NEBCA Platform

High-fidelity enterprise governance, compliance and operational assurance prototype.

## Build 001 — Foundation

The current branch establishes the NEBCA browser prototype with:

- secure-style demo login
- executive dashboard
- industry overlay selector with active workspace routing
- responsive enterprise navigation
- Governance Control Centre
- Decision Register
- Delegations of Authority
- Policy Governance
- Ownership, Control & Strategic Asset Governance
- Action Accountability
- Board Assurance
- Compliance Control Centre
- Obligation & Condition Register
- Licence & Permit Register
- Evidence Vault
- Regulatory Returns
- CAPA
- Compliance Calendar
- full Risk Control Centre with register, controls, treatments, emerging risk, heatmap and appetite
- full Audit Control Centre with programme, workspace, findings, verification, analytics and audit trail
- full SSAL drill-down workflows
- full ISO SHEC SMS drill-down workflows
- deep Mining / GMCDS overlay
- Mining Licence Control
- Production & Dispatch Governance
- Mining Environmental Control
- Mine SHEC
- Contractor & Site Access
- Mine Regulator Pack
- Community & Social Governance
- Mining Asset & Inspection Governance
- Mining Executive View
- regulator workbench and sealed evidence exposure
- Namibia identity and `Designed in Namibia` branding
